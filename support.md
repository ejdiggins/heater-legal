## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap your **avatar on the Home screen**, then **Delete account**.

This permanently removes your profile, picks, guesses, game entries, and login.
It cannot be undone. Games you created are removed along with your account,
including for the other players in them.

If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.

### Is there real money involved?

No. Heater has no deposits, no withdrawals, no cash prizes, and nothing to buy.
Odds shown in the app are reference data used to score predictions — you are not
placing a bet.

### How do I join a friend's game?

Ask them for the game's invite code, then use **Join by Code** on that game's
screen.

### Something looks wrong with my score

Scores settle automatically once the real games finish, which can take a few
hours after the final whistle. If a result still looks wrong the next day, email
us with the game name and we'll look.

### How do I report a player?

Email us with the username and what happened.
