# Privacy Policy for Heater

**Last updated: 26 August 2026**

Heater ("we", "us") is a social sports-prediction game. This policy explains what
we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Heater involves **no real-money wagering**. We do not process payments, and we
never collect financial or payment information.

---

## What we collect

### Information you give us

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | To create your account and sign you in |
| **Password** | Stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider — we never see it |
| **Username** | Shown to other players on leaderboards and in games you join |
| **Profile details** (bio, avatar selection) | Optional, shown to other players |

### Information created by playing

Your picks, guesses, rosters, game entries, scores, and streaks. This is the
substance of the app: leaderboards cannot work without it, and it is visible to
other players in games you join.

### Information collected automatically

We keep a first-party activity log to understand how the app is used — which
screens people open and which actions they complete. Each record contains:

- The name of the action (for example, `daily_pick_locked`)
- Related details about that action, such as which game type it belonged to
- A **session identifier**, randomly generated each time the app launches and
  never reused
- The platform and app version
- Your account identifier, when you are signed in

Broadly, this covers **the actions you take in the app, which features you use,
and how often you return**. As Heater grows we expect to log more of this kind
of product-usage information. It is always about how the app is used, and it
stays within the categories described here.

We do **not** use advertising identifiers, and we do not build a persistent
device fingerprint. We do not track you across other companies' apps or
websites.

---

## What we do **not** collect

- Location data of any kind
- Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
- Payment or financial information
- Health data
- Browsing or search history from outside the app
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
- Biometric data

Heater requests no device permissions beyond what iOS requires to run.

---

## How we use it

- To operate the app: accounts, games, scoring, leaderboards
- To show other players your username and results in games you are both in
- To understand which features are used, so we can improve them
- To investigate abuse, cheating, or technical faults
- To meet legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** share it with
advertisers. We do **not** use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

---

## Who else is involved

| Provider | Role |
|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosts our database and handles authentication. Your account and gameplay data are stored there. |
| **Expo (EAS)** | Delivers app updates. When your app checks for one, Expo receives your IP address and app version. |
| **Apple** | Distributes the app and provides TestFlight. Governed by Apple's own privacy policy. |

We use **SportsGameOdds** for sports data. Those requests are made by our servers
and contain **no information about you**.

## If Heater is acquired

If we are ever bought, merged with another company, or sell part of the business,
the information described in this policy may be **transferred as part of that
transaction**, along with the rest of the company. Any acquirer would receive it
subject to this policy.

We would tell you in the app before your information became subject to a
materially different privacy policy, so you can delete your account first if you
prefer.

This is a transfer of the business, not a sale of your personal information to a
third party, and it does not change the commitments above: we do not sell your
data, share it with advertisers, or use it for cross-app tracking.

---

## Other players

Anything visible in a shared game — your username, picks once revealed, scores,
and standings — is visible to the other players in that game. Do not put
anything private in a username or bio.

---

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we
delete it (see below). Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be
retained.

---

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: **tap your avatar on the Home
screen → Delete account**.

This permanently removes your profile, your picks and guesses, your game entries,
and your login. It cannot be undone. Games you created are removed along with
your account, including for other players in them.

If you cannot access the app, email us at the address below and we will delete
your account on request.

---

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete
your data, to object to certain processing, or to receive a copy of it. Contact us
and we will respond.

Residents of California, the EEA, and the UK have specific rights under the CCPA
and GDPR. We do not sell personal information as those laws define it.

---

## Children

Heater is not intended for children. The app is rated for mature audiences
because it simulates sports-prediction contests, and we do not knowingly collect
information from anyone under the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child
has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

---

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with our
hosting provider using their security controls. No system is perfectly secure,
but we do not store payment details, and passwords are never stored in a form we
can read.

---

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes
will be announced in the app.
